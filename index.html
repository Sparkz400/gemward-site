Support

Gemward is a maze-building tower defense game for Windows, released on Steam. If something goes wrong, write to us and we will help.

Email: devstudiosNS@googlemail.com

Please write in English or German. The Steam discussion forum opens with the store page.

What to include

  • The game version, if you know it.
  • What you were doing and what happened.
  • Your log file, if the game crashed or misbehaved: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Gemward\Gemward\Player.log

Common questions

Where are my saves?

In %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Gemward\Gemward\. With Steam Cloud on, your profiles also sync between your computers.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Solo play works in Steam's offline mode. Achievements earned offline are sent the next time Steam is online. Multiplayer, the Workshop and leaderboards need a connection.

Does Gemward support controllers?

Yes, with partial controller support: menus and matches can be played with a gamepad, using a virtual pointer where needed.

What happens after a crash?

The next time you start, Gemward asks whether to send a crash report. Nothing is sent unless you agree. See the privacy policy for what a report contains.

Can I remove my leaderboard entries?

Yes. Write to us with your Steam profile link and we will remove them.
