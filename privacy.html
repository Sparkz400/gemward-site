Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

Gemward has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game sends data to us only in one case: a crash report you have agreed to send. Everything else stays on your computer or is handled by Steam.

1. Who is responsible

Nico Schürmann
Email: devstudiosNS@googlemail.com

2. Data stored on your computer

Gemward keeps your profiles, progress, records, settings and suspended runs in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Gemward\Gemward\. Your profiles hold your Steam ID and Steam display name so that each Steam account keeps its own saves. These files are not sent to us.

Unity's engine analytics, hardware statistics and diagnostics are switched off in the game.

3. Steam

Gemward is sold and run through Steam, operated by Valve Corporation. Valve processes your account, purchase and gameplay data under its own Steam Privacy Policy. The following game features use Steam:

  • Steam Cloud copies your profile files to Valve's servers so they are available on your other computers.
  • Achievements and stats are stored by Steam and shown on your Steam profile according to your Steam privacy settings.
  • Multiplayer and chat connect players through Steam's networking. Other players in your session see your Steam name, your court and the chat messages you send. We do not store chat.
  • Steam Workshop content you publish from the Masons' Lodge is public according to the visibility you choose.

4. Community leaderboards (only if you opt in)

If you turn on leaderboard publishing, Gemward uploads the results of eligible runs to Steam leaderboards: your score and the run's details (mode, difficulty, court, wave and time values, the run's seed and the game version). Anyone can see these entries together with your Steam name.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can turn publishing off at any time in the game's settings. To have existing entries removed, email us with your Steam profile link.

5. Crash reports (only if you agree)

When Gemward crashes or runs into an error, it asks whether to send a report. You can answer Don't send, Send or Always send, and change this later in Settings → Company → Crash reports. Choosing Never send also deletes reports that are waiting.

A report contains:

  • the error message, the program's stack trace and the last part of the game's log file;
  • technical details: game version, mode, difficulty, court, round, operating system, graphics card and memory size;
  • after a crash, the crash dump file (a snapshot of the game's memory, at most 20 MB).

Before a report is saved, Gemward removes Steam IDs, your Windows user folder and user name, email addresses, your profile name, your Steam display name and your computer's name from its text. The crash dump is binary and is sent as captured, so it may incidentally contain other data that was in the game's memory.

Reports go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which processes them for us as a data processor in its EU data region in Germany. When a report is sent, Sentry technically receives your IP address; we do not use it to identify you. We use reports only to find and fix bugs and delete them after at most 90 days.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the setting above; this does not affect reports already sent.

6. Support emails

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you. The mailbox is hosted by Google. We delete support emails once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to deliver the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use these rights, email us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If the game starts collecting different data, we will update this page and the date above.
